Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Alláh-u-Abhá Counter does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts. There is no sign-in, and the app never asks who you are.
  • No data collection. The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting.
  • No internet connection. The app works fully offline and never sends anything over the internet.
  • No access to your personal data. The app does not use your contacts, location, photos, camera, or microphone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your current count and your melody setting are saved on your iPhone, so you can pick up where you left off. They never leave your device, and deleting the app removes them.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
