Alláh-u-Abhá Counter

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Alláh-u-Abhá Counter does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts. There is no sign-in, and the app never asks who you are.
  • No data collection. The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting.
  • No internet connection. The app works fully offline and never sends anything over the internet.
  • No access to your personal data. The app does not use your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. Location is used only as described below.

Location, for the Qiblih compass

The Qiblih compass points toward the Shrine of Bahá'u'lláh at Bahjí. To work out that direction, it needs to know roughly where you are, so the first time you open the compass, your iPhone asks for permission to use your location.

  • Your location is used only on your iPhone, to calculate the direction and distance to the Qiblih.
  • It is never saved, never sent anywhere, and never shared.
  • The app uses your location only while the compass is open, and stops as soon as you close it.
  • An approximate location is enough, so the compass works even with Precise Location turned off.
  • You can turn location off for the app anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. The counter works the same without it.

Notifications, for the reminders

The app can remind you to recite Alláh-u-Abhá each day, and to say your obligatory prayer. Both reminders are off unless you turn them on.

  • When you turn one on, your iPhone asks for permission to show notifications.
  • The reminders are scheduled on your iPhone itself. Nothing is sent anywhere, and no server is involved.
  • You can turn them off anytime in the app's ⓘ screen, or in Settings → Notifications.

What stays on your iPhone

Your current count and your settings (melody, appearance, text size, and reminders) are saved on your iPhone, so you can pick up where you left off. They never leave your device, and deleting the app removes them.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy
