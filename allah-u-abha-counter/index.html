Alláh-u-Abhá Counter

A calm counter for Bahá'ís reciting Alláh-u-Abhá 95 times each day.

Count without looking

The whole screen is the button. Tap anywhere to count from 1 to 95. At 95, your iPhone vibrates, a short chime plays, and the screen glows softly like sunrise.

  • A Qiblih compass points toward the Shrine of Bahá'u'lláh at Bahjí, for your obligatory prayers.
  • The chime follows your Ring/Silent setting, or you can set it to always play or turn it off.
  • The screen stays on while you count.
  • Your count is saved on your iPhone.
  • Works fully offline and collects no information.
