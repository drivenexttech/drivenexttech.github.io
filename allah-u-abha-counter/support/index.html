A calm counter for reciting Alláh-u-Abhá 95 times each day.

How to use it

  • Count: tap anywhere on the screen. Each tap adds one, from 1 to 95.
  • At 95: the phone vibrates, a short chime plays, and the screen glows. Each tap after 95 repeats this, and the count stays at 95.
  • Start over: press and hold Hold to Reset at the bottom of the screen until the gold bar fills.
  • Find the Qiblih: tap the compass button at the top right. Hold your phone flat and turn until the gold star reaches the marker at the top. When you're facing the Qiblih, the screen glows and your phone gives a gentle tap.
  • Sound: tap the speaker in the top-right corner to choose how the chime plays:
    • Follow Ring/Silent (the default): quiet when your iPhone is on silent, plays when it's on ring.
    • Always Play: plays even when your iPhone is on silent.
    • Off: no chime. The vibration at 95 still happens.
  • Prayers: tap the book button at the top left. The verses said while washing the hands and face are right at the top, and below them are the Short, Medium, and Long Obligatory Prayers. Under In Place of the Obligatory Prayer are the verse for women in their courses, with a button to count its 95 repetitions on the counter, and the prostration for obligatory prayers missed when it wasn't safe to pray. Everything is in the app, so it works without an internet connection. While reading a prayer, use the A− and A+ buttons at the top right to change the text size.
  • About the practice: tap ⓘ at the top left to read about the daily recitation.
  • Reminders: in the ⓘ screen, under Reminders:
    • Alláh-u-Abhá, 95 times: one gentle notification each day at the time you choose.
    • Obligatory prayer: choose Short, Medium, or Long and set the time. Medium has three times: morning, noon, and evening. Tap the reminder to open that prayer.
  • Sunset look: in the ⓘ screen, under Appearance, choose Sunset for a calm evening-blue sky that's softer on the eyes at night, Sunrise to always keep the light colors, or Automatic to follow your iPhone's Light and Dark setting.

Common questions

I don't hear the chime at 95.

If the speaker is set to Follow Ring/Silent, the chime stays quiet while your iPhone is on silent. Switch your iPhone to ring, or tap the speaker and choose Always Play. Also check that your volume is turned up.

I don't feel the vibration at 95.

On your iPhone, open Settings → Sounds & Haptics and make sure Haptics is set to Always Play.

The Qiblih compass asks for my location. Why?

To point toward the Shrine of Bahá'u'lláh, the compass needs to know roughly where you are. Your location is used only on your iPhone and is never saved or shared. If you said no earlier, turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

The compass seems off or keeps moving.

Hold your phone flat and away from magnets, metal, and other electronics. If the app suggests it, move your phone in a figure eight to recalibrate the compass. For the best accuracy, make sure Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → System Services → Compass Calibration is on.

My reminder doesn't appear.

Open Settings → Notifications → Alláh-u-Abhá and make sure Allow Notifications is on. A Focus mode such as Sleep or Do Not Disturb can also hold notifications until it ends.

Why is the app blue?

With Appearance set to Automatic (the default), the app follows your iPhone: when your iPhone is in Dark Mode, the app uses its evening-blue Sunset look. To always see the sunrise colors, tap ⓘ and choose Sunrise.

Will my count be saved if I close the app?

Yes. Your count is saved on your iPhone and is still there when you come back. Deleting the app clears it.

Does the screen turn off while I'm counting?

No. The screen stays on while you count, so it won't dim in the middle of your prayers.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. It works fully offline, including the Qiblih compass, and collects no information. See the privacy policy.
